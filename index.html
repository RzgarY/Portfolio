About

I’m a game designer and 3D artist from Türkiye. I like building stylized visuals out of clean, low-to-mid-poly assets, and I work in Unity for modeling, texturing and basic animation.

Racing games are my favorite playground: how a car feels, how a lap reads, how quickly someone can pick it up. I aim for projects that are polished, fun to play and easy to understand.
